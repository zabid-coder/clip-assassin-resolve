Highlighter Pro – ReadMe.txt

⭐ 1. What is Highlighter Pro?
Highlighter Pro is a custom DaVinci Resolve plugin designed for editors, documentary creators, and content makers who work with articles, documents, or reports.

Highlight quotes, statistics, and important details instantly — directly in the Edit Page or inside Fusion for more control.
NOTE: Works only in Davinci Resolve 20+

⭐ 2. What’s included:


- Fully customizable colors, opacity, texturing & blend modes

- Works in Edit Page (optimized) and Fusion (single-instance recommended)

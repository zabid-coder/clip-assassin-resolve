NeoFX Core Pack – v2.0 Update

The biggest Core Pack update yet, 7 brand new effects and a restructured pack.

What's new:

Neo Shatter (Fuse)
- Explode text and graphics into customizable fragments
- Full control over fragmentation, direction, and animation

Neo Bevel (Macro)
- 3D beveled edges with adjustable highlights and shadows
- Works on text, shapes, and graphics

Neo Chroma (Fuse)
- Chromatic aberration with RGB channel splitting
- Adjustable intensity and angle for cinematic looks

Neo Lens Distort (Fuse)
- Barrel and pincushion lens distortion
- Adds real camera energy to motion graphics

Neo Glitch (Fuse)
- Scan lines, color shift, and block displacement
- Fully animated with customizable parameters

Neo Tri-Tone (Fuse)
- Map three custom colors to shadows, midtones, and highlights
- Instant stylized color grading

Neo Long Shadow (Fuse)
- Extended directional shadows with color and angle control
- Perfect for bold, graphic text styles

Changes:

- Pack now contains 15 effects: 9 Macros + 6 Fuses
- Macros (9): available as both DRFX (Edit Page) and .setting files (Fusion Page)
- Fuses (6): available in the Fusion Page only, installed to your Fuses folder

Full effect list:

Macros: Neo Light Sweep Pro, Neo Glow Pro, Neo Reflection, Neo Halftone, Neo Inner Glow, Neo Emboss, Neo Inner Shadow, Neo Paper Curl, Neo Bevel
Fuses: Neo Shatter, Neo Chroma, Neo Lens Distort, Neo Glitch, Neo Tri-Tone, Neo Long Shadow
